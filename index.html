TileCity

A tile-laying game of expanding roads and cities to score points.

Play Rules

Add to Home Screen

Add TileCity to your Home Screen from Safari’s Share menu and play it like an app!

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom (or in the toolbar).
  3. Scroll and choose “Add to Home Screen”, then tap Add.
  4. →
    Add to Home Screen

Add TileCity from Chrome’s browser menu!

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Choose “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”, then confirm.
  4. →
    Add to Home screen

Menu names vary by browser and phone maker.
