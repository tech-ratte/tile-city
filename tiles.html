TileCity Play

Rules

Your turn

  • Draw one tile and place it adjacent to tiles already on the board. Every touching edge must match.
  • You may place one meeple on a city, road, monastery, or grassland on the tile you just placed.
  • You cannot place a meeple on a feature that already has any meeple — even far along the same connected area.
  • A finished city, road, or monastery scores right away, and those meeples return to their owners.
  • A meeple laid on grassland stays until the end of the game.

Notes

Extras the tutorial doesn’t cover — placement limits, controls, and a scoring cheat sheet.

How to place tiles

  • Share a full side The new tile must touch an existing tile along at least one full side. Touching only at a corner does not count.
  • Matching edges Where tiles touch, the terrain must continue: city to city, road to road, field to field.
  • Rotate only You may rotate the tile, but you cannot flip or mirror it.

OK example

Center is start tile D. North city matches E rotated 180°. East road matches U rotated 90°. South field matches V’s north field.

Not allowed — edges clash

D’s east edge is a road, but unrotated E’s west edge is a field. Even one mismatched side means you cannot place it there.

Not allowed — corner only

Tiles that only meet at a corner do not share a side, so you cannot place them that way.

Meeples

  • Where you can place Only on the tile you just placed — on a free road, city, monastery, or field mark.
  • No joining occupied areas You cannot place a meeple on a road, city, or field that already has any meeple somewhere in its connected area.
  • Running out If you have no meeples left, place the tile only. Completing a road, city, or monastery returns those meeples (except during final scoring).

Scoring during the game

When a road, city, or monastery is completed, the player with the most meeples on it scores. If tied for most, each tied player scores the full points. Those meeples then return to their owners.

AreaWhen completed
Road1 point per tile in the road
City2 points per tile + 2 per pennant (banner)
Monastery9 points when all 8 surrounding spaces are filled
FieldNot scored during the game

After the last tile is placed (or you Exit early), final scoring runs.

  • Incomplete roads 1 point per tile.
  • Incomplete cities 1 point per tile + 1 per pennant.
  • Incomplete monasteries 1 point per filled space in the 3×3 area (including the monastery tile).
  • Fields Farmers stay on the board. Each field scores 3 points for every completed city it touches. Majority works the same as above.

Controls

  • Rotate Right-click the board, press R while the pointer is over the board, or tap the hand preview / angle thumbs.
  • Zoom Mouse wheel or pinch. Buttons on desktop legend.
  • Pan Drag the board.
  • Hints Green = can place now. Gray = needs a different rotation.

Separate cities on tile I

Tile I has two cities that only meet at a corner. They stay separate unless each connects to other tiles along a full city edge.
