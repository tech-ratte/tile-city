Goal

Score the most points by completing roads, cities, and monasteries during the game, then score leftover features and fields at the end.

Setup

  • Players 2–8. Each color has 7 meeples. Pick names (max 6 letters) and unique colors on the setup screen.
  • Start tile One marked D tile starts face-up in the center. Shuffle the other 71 tiles into the deck.
  • First player The starting player is chosen at random. Turns then follow the player list order.

On your turn

  • 1. Draw Take the top tile so everyone can see it.
  • 2. Place Rotate in 90° steps and place it legally (see Placement). If no orientation fits anywhere, discard it and draw again — your turn is not spent.
  • 3. Meeple (optional) You may put one of your meeples on an open feature of the tile you just placed.
  • 4. Score Any roads, cities, or monasteries completed by that placement score now and return those meeples.

Placement

  • Touch a side The new tile must share at least one full side with an existing tile. Corner-only contact is illegal.
  • Edges match Every touching side must match end-to-end: city–city, road–road, field–field.
  • No flip Rotate only; never mirror the tile.
  • Open board The map can grow in any direction.

Legal

Center is start tile D. North city matches E rotated 180°. East road matches U rotated 90°. South field matches V’s north field.

Illegal — edges clash

D’s east edge is a road; unrotated E’s west edge is a field. One mismatch blocks the place.

Illegal — corner only

Tiles that only touch at a corner do not share a side, so the place is illegal.

Meeples

  • Where Only on the tile you just placed: road, city, monastery, or field mark that is still free.
  • One feature You cannot join a road, city, or field that already has any meeple on its connected area.
  • Stock If you have no meeples left, skip placement. Completing a feature returns your meeples there (except at final scoring).
  • Skip You may place the tile and skip the meeple.

Scoring during the game

When a feature finishes, the player(s) with the most meeples on it score. Ties: every tied player gets the full points. Those meeples return to stock.

FeatureComplete
Road1 point per tile in the road
City2 points per tile + 2 per pennant (banner)
Monastery9 points when all 8 neighbors are filled
FieldNot scored mid-game

Game end

  • When After the last tile is placed (or Exit ends the game early), final scoring runs.
  • Incomplete roads 1 point per tile.
  • Incomplete cities 1 point per tile + 1 per pennant.
  • Incomplete monasteries 1 point per filled cell in the 3×3 (including the monastery tile).
  • Fields Farmers stay on the board. Each field scores 3 points per completed city it touches. Majority rules as above.
  • Meeples Final scoring does not return meeples; +N labels show what each scored.
  • Ties Equal score → more meeples still in stock ranks higher.

Controls

  • Rotate Right-click the board, press R while the pointer is over the board, or tap the hand preview / angle thumbs.
  • Zoom Mouse wheel or pinch. Buttons on desktop legend.
  • Pan Drag the board.
  • Hints Green = can place now. Gray = needs a different rotation.

Separate cities on tile I

Tile I has two cities that only meet at a corner point. They stay separate unless each joins other tiles along a full city edge.

Joins the north city

E rotated 180° matches I’s north city edge. I’s west city stays separate.

Joins the west city

E rotated 90° matches I’s west city edge. I’s north city stays separate.

Still two cities

Even with both sides filled, I’s cities remain split by field inside the tile. Corner-only contact between the two E tiles does not merge them.
