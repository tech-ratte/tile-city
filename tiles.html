TileCity

Rules

Play

Your turn

  • Draw one tile and place it adjacent to tiles already on the board. Every touching edge must match.
  • You may place one meeple on a city, road, monastery, or grassland on the tile you just placed.
  • You cannot place a meeple on a feature that already has any meeple — even far along the same connected area.
  • A finished city, road, or monastery scores right away, and those meeples return to their owners.
  • A meeple laid on grassland stays until the end of the game.
